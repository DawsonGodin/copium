# Content Policy

*Effective September 26, 2026*

> A copy of [copium.pics/content-policy](https://copium.pics/content-policy) - the version there is the official one.

<p>These rules apply to every image, video (including its audio), title and file name on Copium. They add to the <a href="TERMS.md">Terms of Service</a> and to the rules of wherever you share your uploads. If you're unsure, don't upload it.</p>

## 1. Zero tolerance: child safety

<p>Never upload <strong>any sexual or sexualised content involving minors</strong>: anyone under 18, or anyone who appears or is described as under 18. This includes drawings, animation, AI-generated images, "fictional" or roleplay characters, and "age play". Breaking this rule means a permanent ban, and we preserve the content and report it to NCMEC and/or law enforcement. There are no warnings, and once staff confirm a violation it can't be appealed.</p>

## 2. Never allowed

<p><ul>
<li><strong>Sexual violence:</strong> any depiction of sexual assault, rape or non-consensual sexual activity, whether real, staged, drawn or part of a roleplay scene.</li>
<li><strong>Sexual content and nudity</strong> of any kind, including sexually suggestive images. Euphoric doesn't host adult content.</li>
<li><strong>Intimate images of real people</strong> shared without their consent, including "deepfakes" and edited images.</li>
<li><strong>Real-world gore</strong>, graphic injury, or footage of real violence, death or animal cruelty.</li>
<li><strong>Self-harm or suicide</strong> content that shows, encourages or instructs it.</li>
<li><strong>Hate and extremism:</strong> content that attacks people for protected characteristics, hate symbols used to promote hate, and terrorist or violent extremist material.</li>
<li><strong>Harassment and threats</strong> against anyone, including other members and staff.</li>
<li><strong>Personal information (doxxing):</strong> anyone's real name, address, phone number, face, or other real-life identifying details without their consent.</li>
<li><strong>Illegal goods or activity:</strong> anything that promotes real-world illegal activity, or anything that's illegal where we operate.</li>
<li><strong>Content you don't have the rights to</strong> (see the <a href="COPYRIGHT.md">Copyright Policy</a>).</li>
<li><strong>Spam, scams or impersonation</strong> of staff, members, or other people and organisations.</li>
</ul></p>

## 3. Roleplay context

<p>In-game screenshots of roleplay scenes, including police chases, shootouts and crime scenes, are generally fine if they aren't graphic and don't break any rule above. Fiction is not an exception to the rules on minors, sexual content or sexual violence.</p>

## 4. How we enforce this

<p><ul>
<li>Every upload is scanned automatically before it's published. Anything that appears to break these rules is rejected and counts as a strike.</li>
<li><strong>3 strikes within 24 hours</strong> automatically suspends your account until staff review it.</li>
<li>Anyone can report an image. Reports about minors or sexual violence hide the image straight away.</li>
<li>Staff can remove content and warn, suspend or permanently ban accounts. Removed images are fingerprinted so they can't be uploaded again.</li>
<li>Trying to get around a ban or the scanner (for example with another account, or by cropping or editing images) results in a permanent ban.</li>
</ul></p>

## 5. Appeals

<p>If your account is suspended, sign in with Discord to see the reason and send an appeal. Staff review every appeal. Automated suspensions can always be appealed. Bans that staff have confirmed for child-safety violations cannot. You can also email <a href="mailto:legal@copium.pics">legal@copium.pics</a>.</p>

## 6. Reporting

<p>Use the "Report this image" link on any image page. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. You can report child sexual abuse material directly to <a href="https://report.cybertip.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NCMEC's CyberTipline</a> (US) or the <a href="https://report.iwf.org.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Internet Watch Foundation</a> (UK/international).</p>
