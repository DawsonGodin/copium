# Privacy Policy

*Effective September 26, 2026*

> A copy of [copium.pics/privacy](https://copium.pics/privacy) - the version there is the official one.

<p>This policy explains what Copium, a public image and video host, collects, why, and what your choices are. The service is run by Dawson Godin, who controls this data. Contact: <a href="mailto:legal@copium.pics">legal@copium.pics</a>.</p>

## 1. What we collect

<p><ul>
<li><strong>From Discord when you sign in:</strong> your Discord user ID, username or display name (or your server nickname), and avatar. We store your role IDs in the server and re-check them regularly while you're signed in (using our Discord bot), so access ends if you leave or lose a role. We never receive your Discord password or email address.</li>
<li><strong>Your uploads:</strong> the image or video, a thumbnail, the title, the original file name, the file size, the dimensions, the length of videos, the upload time and fingerprints of the file. <strong>Location and camera data (EXIF/GPS and video metadata) is removed</strong> before anything is stored.</li>
<li><strong>Safety records:</strong> scan results, blocked uploads, reports, removals, suspensions and bans, and the date you accepted the rules.</li>
<li><strong>Reports:</strong> the reason and any details you give. If you aren't signed in, we store a keyed one-way hash of your IP address instead of the IP itself, to prevent abuse.</li>
<li><strong>ShareX upload tokens:</strong> if you set up ShareX, we store a one-way hash of your token and when it was created and last used.</li>
<li><strong>Upload IP address:</strong> the IP address each image was uploaded from, kept for 90 days to investigate abuse and illegal content, then erased (kept longer only for content preserved for law enforcement).</li>
<li><strong>Sign-in sessions:</strong> a record of your active sessions so they can be ended when you sign out or lose access.</li>
<li><strong>Appeals:</strong> if your account is suspended and you appeal, your message and the staff decision.</li>
<li><strong>Technical data:</strong> your IP address is used briefly for rate limiting and may appear in short-lived server or hosting logs.</li>
</ul></p>

## 2. How we use it

<p>We use this data to sign you in, host and show your images, enforce storage and rate limits, keep the Service safe (scanning, reports, strikes and bans), meet legal obligations, and respond to your requests. Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, our legal bases are: <em>performing our agreement with you</em> (running the Service); <em>legitimate interests</em> (safety, preventing abuse, protecting our users); and <em>legal obligation</em> (for example, preserving and reporting illegal content).</p>

## 3. Who we share it with

<p>We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. We share it only with:<ul>
<li><strong>Discord</strong>, which handles sign-in.</li>
<li><strong>OpenAI</strong>, whose moderation service scans each image (or sampled video frames) and title when you upload or rename it. This data is processed under OpenAI's API terms.</li>
<li><strong>Our storage, CDN and hosting providers</strong> (for example Cloudflare), which store and deliver images.</li>
<li><strong>Authorities</strong>: NCMEC, law enforcement or courts, where the law requires it or where someone's safety is at risk.</li>
</ul>Some of these providers are in the United States, so your data may be processed outside your country.</p>

## 4. What is public

<p>Anyone with an image's link can see the image. Share pages also show its title, your display name, your avatar and the upload date. Staff can see all uploads and your Discord ID.</p>

## 5. How long we keep it

<p><ul>
<li><strong>Images</strong> stay until you or staff delete them. Deleted files are removed from storage promptly, though cached copies (on CDNs or in other people's browsers) can take a short time to expire.</li>
<li><strong>Fingerprints of removed images</strong> are kept so the same file can't be uploaded again.</li>
<li><strong>Upload IP addresses</strong> are erased after 90 days.</li>
<li><strong>Sessions</strong> expire after 14 days, or immediately when you sign out.</li>
<li><strong>Safety records</strong> (moderation log, reports, bans, appeals) are kept for as long as needed to keep the Service safe and meet legal requirements.</li>
<li><strong>Content preserved for law enforcement</strong> (for example suspected CSAM) is kept privately for 365 days and then deleted automatically, unless authorities ask us to keep it longer (a "legal hold").</li>
<li><strong>Images removed for copyright</strong> are kept privately for a short period so they can be restored if you send a valid counter-notice, then deleted.</li>
<li><strong>Database backups</strong> are kept for about two weeks and are private.</li>
</ul></p>

## 6. Cookies and storage

<p>We use one essential cookie that keeps you signed in, for up to 14 days. Your browser also remembers your preferred link format, which never leaves your device. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.</p>

## 7. Your choices and rights

<p>You can rename or delete any of your images at any time, download a copy of your data, and delete your account and all of its images, all from the "My uploads" page. Suspended members can do the same from the sign-in page. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to or restrict how it's processed. To make a request, email <a href="mailto:legal@copium.pics">legal@copium.pics</a>. We may need to keep some records even after deletion where the law requires it, for safety reasons (such as bans) or because of an active investigation. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.</p>

## 8. Children

<p>The Service is not for anyone under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child is using the Service, contact <a href="mailto:legal@copium.pics">legal@copium.pics</a> and we'll remove the account.</p>

## 9. Security

<p>We use HTTPS, give staff limited access, and keep images under review or preserved for law enforcement in private storage that isn't public. No system is completely secure, so please don't upload sensitive images.</p>

## 10. Changes

<p>If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date, and we'll announce significant changes in the Service or on Discord.</p>
