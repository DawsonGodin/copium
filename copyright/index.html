Copyright Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

A copy of copium.pics/copyright - the version there is the official one.

We respect intellectual property rights and respond to notices of alleged infringement in line with the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), 17 U.S.C. § 512, and similar laws.

1. Submitting a takedown notice

If you believe an image on the Service infringes your copyright, email our designated agent at copyright@copium.pics. Your notice must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  3. The link to each image you want removed (for example https://copium.pics/i/abc12345).
  4. Your name, address, phone number and email address.
  5. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use isn't authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or are authorised to act for the owner.

2. What happens next

When we receive a valid notice, we remove the image from public access, record a copyright strike against the uploader's account, and keep a private copy for a short period in case of a counter-notice. Anyone who knowingly makes a false claim may be liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

3. Counter-notices

If your image was removed and you believe that was a mistake or misidentification, you can send a counter-notice to copyright@copium.pics. It must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. The image link, and a description of the image, as it appeared before it was removed.
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith that the image was removed by mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, address and phone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if you're outside the US, any judicial district where we can be found), and that you'll accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.
We'll forward the counter-notice to the person who sent the original notice. Unless they tell us within 10 to 14 business days that they've filed a court action, we may restore the image.

4. Repeat infringers

We terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of members who repeatedly infringe copyright. 3 valid notices against one account (not counting ones later withdrawn or successfully counter-noticed) lead to a permanent ban.

5. Game and third-party content

Screenshots of games can contain material owned by the game's publisher. Trademarks and game content belong to their respective owners. We aren't affiliated with them, and we honour valid notices from rights holders.
